Our History

Our Pokémon Event Center began as a small passion project created by a group of dedicated trainers who wanted a better way to organize and share local events. What started as a simple website listing a few meetups and casual tournaments quickly grew as more players joined the community. Over time, we expanded to host larger Pokémon TCG tournaments, Pokémon GO raid gatherings, and themed event days, building a reputation for well-organized, welcoming experiences. As the community grew, so did our platform. We introduced event calendars, online registration, and dedicated spaces for players to connect, trade, and prepare for upcoming competitions. Today, our website serves as a central hub for Pokémon fans to discover events, stay updated, and be part of an ever-growing network of trainers. While we’ve come a long way from our early days, our goal remains the same: to bring people together through a shared love of Pokémon and create memorable event experiences for everyone involved.
